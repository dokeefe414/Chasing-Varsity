# v1.0 verification — November 6, 2026

Automated checks use the actual JavaScript engines and UI controllers with a minimal DOM. They are not a human usability study.

- A fresh-save controller playthrough completes all six Varsity calendar meets and earns the national medal, then preserves the final story chapter after save sanitization. Training uses 70% quality, weakest-stat practice, affordable squad workouts and course-specific lineup recommendations. The test caps training at 35 sessions.
- All three alternate drills succeed using normal action/hold mechanics and fail their objective when idle. Countdown/pause stop progress; repeated completion cannot pay twice.
- Recruiting preserves original runners. Four to six selected teammates race alongside the captain. Duplicate/stale imported selections are repaired. A benched partner cannot provide a race benefit; selecting them restores the correct association.
- The first result does not falsely claim a previous personal best.
- Three-circuit deterministic balance runs at 55% / 85% training quality won all 18 meets. First national titles required 18 / 14 sessions; all circuits required 21 / 17. These are specific controller strategies, not promised human completion times.
- Browser checks on November 5 covered the fresh guide, skipping introductions, squad/scouting screen and alternate training. Phone-sized 390 × 844 portrait and 844 × 390 landscape frames were inspected. The landscape trail canvas and action button fit the frame without horizontal overflow.
- These were browser frame checks, not physical iPhone/Android testing. Audio and real-world touch latency remain manual testing opportunities.

Course fit is a dry-weather weighted estimate, not a simulated finishing order. Finish art is an illustration; the existing replay supplies sampled race positions.
