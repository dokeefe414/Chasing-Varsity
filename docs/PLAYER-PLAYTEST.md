# Chasing Varsity: 15-minute player playtest

Status: protocol prepared; no human participants have been observed for this release.

Ask 3–5 people who have not played before to use their own phone or computer. Let them play without coaching for the first five minutes. Do not clear their existing progress: use a fresh browser profile or export a backup first.

1. Start fresh, complete a workout, and enter Pine Hollow. Ask them to say what they expect the controls to do.
2. After the race, ask what caused their result and which action they would change. Note whether they notice energy, passing and the finishing kick.
3. Open Season, choose a strategy, and enter a team meet. Ask which teammate is following which instruction. Can they distinguish their captain from teammates and rivals?
4. Open a rivalry record. Can they explain who won the last duel and whether a rematch matters to them?
5. On a phone, rotate during a paused race, resume, change lanes and finish. Note any accidental taps, clipped text or buttons that cannot be reached.
6. Ask: Would you play another race? What was the best moment? Where did you want to quit? Rate clarity, control and desire for another race from 1–5.

Record device/browser, task, hesitation or failure, exact quote and severity. Avoid collecting personal information. Save observations locally; there is no analytics upload in the game.

Prioritize blockers and accidental actions first, then confusing decisions. Change one balance rule at a time and repeat the same task. Do not infer enjoyment from a scripted win or a passing test.
